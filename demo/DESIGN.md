## Style Prompt

Technical field-notes: dark forest ink, warm paper data, no neon SaaS.

## Colors

- `#1c2b24` ink
- `#f4f1ea` paper
- `#d6f5c8` terminal
- `#c45c26` accent

## Typography

- Source Serif 4 for titles
- IBM Plex Mono for data

## What NOT to Do

- No electric blue
- No stock stock-photo city
- No jump cuts without a fade
